sayMali Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

sayMali teaches Somali. This policy says what the app stores, what leaves your phone, and how to erase it.

What stays on this phone

Your learning lives on this phone: lessons you finish, your streak, hearts, gold bars, review cards, listening minutes, your display name and your settings.

There are no accounts and nothing syncs, so guest progress never leaves the phone.

Your voice

Speaking practice is recorded on this phone so you can hear it back and compare. Practice recordings are not uploaded anywhere.

Adults can opt in to share short voice samples to help improve Somali speech tools. Samples are collected only after you opt in, stay on this phone for now, and are deleted within 30 days. If you turn the option off, the audio is removed from every sample straight away.

Accounts and friends, when they arrive

A later build adds optional accounts. If you create one, your progress and profile are stored in our database (Supabase) so you can pick up on another phone. Friends features store what they need: your display name, your friends, your posts, voice notes and game results.

Voice notes are for friends only, last at most 10 seconds, and are deleted after 7 days unless you save them.

Problem reports you write stay on this phone.

No ads, no selling data

sayMali has no ads and never sells personal data.

The app counts how it is used, lessons finished and streaks kept, with ids and numbers only. It never counts audio, names or free text, and those counts stay on this phone.

Who the app is for

sayMali is for learners aged 13 and older. Learners aged 13 to 17 add friends only by code, link or QR code, and nothing they post is public.

Erasing your data

Reset progress in Settings erases everything on this phone and starts the app over. If you have an account, Delete account in Settings removes it from the server and wipes this phone too. Neither can be undone.
